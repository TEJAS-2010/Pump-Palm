Using Pump Pulse on your phone

  1. Open the farm workspace

    Open the site in your phone browser. Explore the example field, satellite map, rotation comparison and probe readings without a Pump Pulse account. Select العربية to switch the workspace to Arabic.

  2. Save your field

    Tap Use my field, choose your country and drop a pin. Tap Draw boundary and add at least three corners if you want an area estimate. Continue to create an account or sign in. You can add crop details later.

  3. Connect the probe

    Open Arduino & salinity, tap Pair Arduino and save the device token privately. Run the USB bridge on the computer connected to the ESP32. Open the same field on your phone to see the incoming reading. The phone uses the website, while the computer reads the USB cable.

    Open the USB bridge setup ↗

  4. Check the readings

    Open Field overview for local weather and five-day reference water demand when forecast data is available. If a probe is connected, check the reading time here. Open Satellite map to select a NASA layer and date. If the probe reports only raw ADC, the dashboard shows the relative index for this calibrated device.

  5. Compare what to plant next

    Open Rotation planner to compare the current rotation with a legume cover option and a different crop mix. Enter a seasonal water budget and each option’s water estimate. Check planting dates, salinity and machinery before choosing.
